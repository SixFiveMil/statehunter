Client Storage Secrets & High Entropy Test

This page automatically writes simulated credentials to localStorage, sessionStorage, and window globals.

Open Chrome DevTools → StateHunter → Secrets & Storage:

  • Verify that AKIAIOSFODNN7EXAMPLE is flagged with CRITICAL severity.
